# Art Direction

## North Star

Make the app feel like a private travel notebook for two people: warm, tidy, romantic, and practical. The visual language should blend cozy Japanese travel details, onsen calm, Tokyo neighborhood energy, cherry blossoms, and a light city-pop color sense.

Use original cozy hand-painted anime-inspired illustration. Do not directly imitate a named studio, artist, character, or film. The target feeling is gentle, nostalgic, detailed, and human-made.

## Palette

- Sakura: `#F6B3BE`
- Matcha: `#7DAA92`
- Indigo ink: `#3F4A5A`
- Lantern amber: `#F3B35B`
- Ryokan cedar: `#8A5A44`
- Cream paper: `#F8EEDC`
- Sky blue: `#79B8D9`
- Soft red: `#D65F5F`

Use cream as the app base, indigo for readable text, and pink/green/amber as accents. Avoid turning the whole UI into one pink theme; mix warm neutrals and cool blue/green notes.

## Motifs

Good motifs: cherry petals, train tickets, station clocks, lanterns, yukata patterns, onsen steam, folded maps, compact cameras, matcha cups, tiny hearts, city crosswalks, ryokan wood, skyline silhouettes.

Avoid: fake Japanese text, random kanji decoration, religious imagery as filler, brand logos, anime characters from existing works, cluttered kawaii overload.

## Illustration Rules

- Rounded, friendly shapes with imperfect hand-painted texture.
- Soft outlines in indigo or warm brown, not pure black.
- Keep important focal points away from the top-left if a page title or badge will sit there.
- Use no readable text inside generated art unless deliberately designed and reviewed.
- Use illustrations as atmosphere, not as the only way to understand a feature.

## Asset Prompts

Use this prompt pattern for future generated images:

```text
Original cozy hand-painted anime-inspired storybook illustration for a private Japan trip planner. Warm Japanese travel details, soft gouache and watercolor texture, gentle city-pop palette, no named-studio imitation. No readable text, no logos, no watermark.
```
